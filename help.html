Common questions

What age is Chessosaurus for?

It is designed for children aged 6 to 12, and older players are welcome. The Baby Dino, Hatchling Pawn and Parasaur introduce chess gently. From Triceratops upwards the dinosaurs play proper chess: a real challenge for older children, teenagers and grown-ups, with dinosaur or traditional pieces.

Is Chessosaurus real chess?

Yes. Every piece is a dinosaur character, from the Hatchling Pawns to the T. Rex King, but the rules are the real rules of chess, so everything children learn works on any board.

When is Chessosaurus out?

It is in its final review at Google Play, for Android phones and tablets, and at the Amazon Appstore, for Fire tablets. To hear on launch day, follow Chessosaurus on YouTube or Facebook.

Does Chessosaurus have adverts or in-app purchases?

No adverts, no subscription, and one price. On Google Play it is free to try for 3 days, then a grown-up unlocks everything once: £1.99 in the UK, US$2.99 in the United States, C$3.50 in Canada, and a similar price elsewhere. On the Amazon Appstore it is bought once before it is downloaded, with nothing to buy inside the app. Children never see a price or a buy button.

Which devices does it run on?

Android devices with Android 8.0 or later, from Google Play, and Amazon Fire tablets with Fire OS 7 or later, from the Amazon Appstore. iPhone and iPad come next: follow Chessosaurus on YouTube or Facebook to hear when.

Does my child need to be able to read?

Not much. Lessons and quiz questions can be read aloud with a speak button (using a voice installed on the device), the games use picture tiles, and an easy-to-read font is one tap away (Aa). Younger children may like a grown-up nearby for the first lesson.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, and the internet setting starts off. The app itself only goes online to send an optional error report, after a grown-up turns that on. Two-device play uses your home Wi-Fi, only between your own two devices.

How do I change the settings?

Tap ⚙ Settings at the top of the home screen, or ⚙ above the board. Pressing Done takes you straight back to where you were. Important settings are in the For grown-ups tab.

I have forgotten my grown-up PIN. What now?

A grown-up can reset it with the device’s own screen lock, or with a sum and a 24-hour wait. The app says when a PIN has been reset. See the grown-up check.

How do I make the computer easier or harder?

Tap New below the board and choose who to play, from the Baby Dino (plays almost at random) up to the T. Rex King. The line under “Your move” shows your colour and who you are playing, for example “White · Parasaur”.

The 3D board is at an awkward angle. How do I fix it?

Drag to turn the board and pinch to zoom. When it looks right, tap 📌 to save that angle. It is saved for that set of pieces, and ⌖ or a double-tap brings it back.

How do two children play on one device?

Start a new game for two players on this device. They can sit opposite each other, with the far pieces turned to face the far player, or side by side.

How do two children play on two devices?

Choose Two devices on the play screen. A grown-up check comes first, every time. One device shows a code, and a grown-up scans it with the other device. The code is never sent, and every move is encrypted. If the option is greyed out with a Wi-Fi-off sign, two-device play has been switched off in For grown-ups.

Can I stop the quiz between games?

Yes: switch off “Learning quiz between games” in Settings. It never appears between two-player games.

Will progress move to a new device?

No. To protect children’s privacy, players, progress and settings stay on the device and are not included in backups. Saved games are kept in the device’s Documents folder (Android 10 and later), where you can see them in the Files app.

How do I delete saved games?

In Settings › For grown-ups, use “Delete all games”. Players and their progress are kept. Deleting one saved game always asks a grown-up first.
