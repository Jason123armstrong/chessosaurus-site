Common questions

What age is Chessosaurus for?

It is designed for children aged 6 to 12, and older players are welcome. The Baby Dino, Hatchling and Parasaur introduce chess gently. From Triceratops upwards the dinosaurs play proper chess: a real challenge for older children, teenagers and grown-ups, with dinosaur or traditional pieces.

Is Chessosaurus real chess?

Yes. Every piece is a dinosaur character, from the Hatchling Pawns to the T. Rex King, but the rules are the real rules of chess, so everything children learn works on any board.

Does Chessosaurus have adverts or in-app purchases?

No adverts, no subscription and nothing more to buy. On Google Play your family can try everything free for 3 days, then a grown-up can unlock it with one payment, behind the grown-up check. On the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets it is bought once before download. Children never see a price or a buy button.

Can we get it in the United States or Canada?

That is the plan at launch: US$2.99 in the United States and C$3.50 in Canada, from Google Play and the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets. It is made in Scotland, so its English is British English.

When is Chessosaurus out?

Soon: it is coming to Google Play and the Amazon Appstore. To hear on launch day, follow Chessosaurus on YouTube or Facebook.

How much is it?

£1.99 once in the UK (US$2.99 in the United States, C$3.50 in Canada). On Google Play your family can try everything free for 3 days, then a grown-up can unlock it with one payment. On the Amazon Appstore it is bought once before download, with no trial. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy, and children never see a price or a buy button.

Which phones and tablets does it run on?

Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later, from Google Play, and Amazon Fire tablets with Fire OS 7 or later, from the Amazon Appstore. iPhone and iPad come next: follow Chessosaurus on YouTube or Facebook to hear when. It is made for children aged 6 to 12, with stronger levels and traditional pieces for older players and grown-ups.

If we get a new phone or tablet, do we pay again?

No. On Google Play the unlock belongs to your Google account, so it comes back on a new device. Children's players and progress stay on the old device, to protect their privacy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases on Google Play follow Google Play's refund policy: for the first 48 hours you can ask for a refund in Google Play itself. After that, or for the Amazon Appstore, email us at support@cumbernauldsoftwaresolutions.com and we will help.

Does my child need to be able to read?

Not much. Lessons and quiz questions can be read aloud with a speak button, the games use picture tiles, and an easy-to-read font is one tap away (Aa). Younger children may like a grown-up nearby for the first lesson.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, and the internet setting starts off. The app itself only goes online to send an optional error report, after a grown-up turns that on. On Google Play, the one purchase is handled by Google Play, which needs the internet at that moment.

How do I change the settings?

Tap ⚙ Settings at the top of the home screen, or ⚙ above the board. Pressing Done takes you straight back to where you were. Important settings are in the For grown-ups tab.

How do I make the computer easier or harder?

Tap New below the board and choose who to play, from the Baby Dino (plays almost at random) up to the T. Rex. The line under “Your move” shows your colour and who you are playing, for example “White · Parasaur”.

The 3D board is at an awkward angle. How do I fix it?

Drag to turn the board and pinch to zoom. When it looks right, tap 📌 to save that angle. It is saved for that set of pieces, and ⌖ or a double-tap brings it back.

How do two children play on two devices?

Choose Two devices on the play screen. One device shows a code; a grown-up scans it with the other device after the grown-up check. If the option is greyed out with a Wi-Fi-off sign, two-device play has been switched off in For grown-ups.

Can I stop the quiz between games?

Yes: switch off “Learning quiz between games” in Settings. It never appears between two-player games.

Will progress move to a new phone?

No. To protect children's privacy, players, progress and saved games stay on the device and are not included in backups.

How do I delete saved games?

In Settings › For grown-ups, use “Delete all games”. Players and their progress are kept.
