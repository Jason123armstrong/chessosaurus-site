How the shop works

  • Run by Teemill. The shop is on Teemill’s website. Teemill takes the order and payment, prints each item to order, and handles delivery, returns and questions, under Teemill’s terms.
  • Organic cotton. The clothes and the tote bag are certified organic cotton.
  • Supports the app. Chessosaurus receives a small share of each sale.
  • Not in the app. The shop is not shown or linked anywhere in the Chessosaurus app.
