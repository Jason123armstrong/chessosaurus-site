No chess? No problem

A one-minute “How to play” starts them off. On the Hatchling board, a tap on Hint shows an arrow for a good move and green squares where a piece is safe. The child still makes the move.

A gentle “Careful!” comes up before a piece is left where it can be taken. When a game doesn’t go their way, the dinosaurs say “Well played!” and point out something they did well.

Learn chess and progress, one dinosaur at a time

Every piece is a dinosaur character, from the Hatchling Pawns to the T. Rex King. The rules are real chess, so everything children learn works on any board.

From a first move to a tricky puzzle

The Dino trail gives a beginner a clear next step: learn a piece, play a little game with it, collect stars, move on.

150 chess puzzles give focused practice once the moves are known.

Seven dinosaurs to beat. Each computer level is a character, not a number, from the Baby Dino who plays almost at random up to the T. Rex.

Chess is just the beginning

21 learning games on picture tiles: languages, maths, dino science, memory and chess skills. There are no timers and no rush, and every round earns stars.

A dino quiz follows the child. It starts gently and adjusts as they answer, and no level or score is ever shown to compare.

Two languages, side by side

Choose a second language and quiz questions, lessons and answers show it right under the first, with a speak button for each, so children see and hear both.

Many languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Chinese (simplified), Japanese and Korean.

The translations were written with AI tools and then checked against the English by several independent AI models. If you spot a mistake, please tell us.

Celebrate progress, at your child’s pace

The Trophy room has 45 stickers to earn for things like finishing a lesson, solving puzzles and trying a new game.

There are no streaks, countdowns or daily reminders. Nothing is lost by putting the tablet down.

A board children can turn to suit them

Play on a flat board, or a 3D one that can be turned, tilted and zoomed. A favourite angle can be saved for each set of pieces.

Two children can play on one device, taking turns. Playing on two devices in the same room, over home Wi-Fi and paired by a grown-up with a code, is new: we are still testing it on more phones and tablets.

And for grown-ups

Don’t think we have forgotten you. Try out the more advanced dino levels, on the dinosaur board or with normal chess pieces.

Seven dinosaurs to beat

Each computer level is a dinosaur. Children see who they are playing, not a number, and move up when they are ready.

Not just for little ones. The first three are gentle teachers for beginners. From Triceratops upwards the dinosaurs play proper chess: a real challenge for older children, teenagers and grown-ups, on the dinosaur board or with traditional pieces.

Made for play, with grown-ups in control.
