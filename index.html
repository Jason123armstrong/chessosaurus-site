Chessosaurus · an Android app for children

Real chess with friendly dinosaurs, for ages 6 to 12.

Short lessons, a path to follow, 150 puzzles and 21 learning games, in twelve languages. Calm by design: no adverts, no accounts, no chat and no tracking.

Pay once. Play as much as you like. No subscriptions and nothing more to buy inside.

Learn a move. Try it on the board.

Every piece is a dinosaur, from the Hatchling pawns to the T. rex king, and each has its own short lesson. Glowing squares show where it can go, and the lesson can be read aloud.

The rules are real chess, so everything a child learns here works on any board, anywhere.

From a first move to a tricky puzzle.

The Dino trail gives a beginner a clear next step: learn a piece, play a little game with it, collect stars, move on.

150 chess puzzles give focused practice once the moves are known.

Seven dinosaurs to beat. Each computer level is a character, not a number, from the Baby dino who plays almost at random up to the T. rex.

  1. Baby dino
  2. Hatchling
  3. Parasaur
  4. Triceratops
  5. Raptor
  6. Queenie
  7. T. rex

Chess is just the beginning.

21 learning games on picture tiles: languages, maths, dino science, memory and chess skills. There are no timers and no rush, and every round earns stars.

A dino quiz follows the child. It starts gently and adjusts as they answer, and no level or score is ever shown to compare.

Two languages, side by side.

Choose a second language and quiz questions, lessons and answers show it right under the first, with a speak button for each, so children see and hear both.

Twelve languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Chinese (simplified), Japanese and Korean.

The translations were written with AI tools and then checked against the English by several independent AI models. If you spot a mistake, please tell us.

Celebrate progress, at your child’s pace.

The Trophy room has 45 stickers to earn for things like finishing a lesson, solving puzzles and trying a new game.

There are no streaks, countdowns or daily reminders. Nothing is lost by putting the tablet down.

A board children can turn to suit them.

Play on a flat board, or a 3D one that can be turned, tilted and zoomed. A favourite angle can be saved for each set of pieces.

Two children can play on one device, or on two devices in the same room over home Wi-Fi, paired by a grown-up with a code.

Made for play, with grown-ups in control.

Chessosaurus is built to be calm and private. Anything that leaves the app, and every important setting, sits behind a grown-up check.

For teachers: it works well on shared tablets, with several players on one device. There is no class dashboard and no sign-in, because the app keeps no accounts.
